nim  about
What this is

An original Napster client, running against a protocol server written from a 2000-era specification and from captures of the client itself. The network is sealed: no page, transfer or message here reaches the internet.

What is real

The client. The protocol. The exhibit pages, which came from the Internet Archive and are served back unmodified, with their original urls intact.

What is not

This page and the museum furniture around it. Any usernames, search results or files the operator chose to fabricate. Where an archived asset was missing it was left missing rather than redrawn. A gap is honest, a guess is not.

Why it matters

Software is not only its source. It is how it felt to use: what loaded first, what the chat looked like, what the site said while the lawsuit was running. That is only legible when the thing still runs.
